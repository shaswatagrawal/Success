Promotion & Counseling Rules

1. Office Counseling Eligibility: Students who visit the Success Education & Visa Services (SEVS) office and complete a counseling session are eligible to spin the wheel.

2. Spin Allocation: Each participant receives exactly 1 verified promotional spin.

3. Instant Prizes: If you land on Rs. 500 Gift Voucher or Rs. 1,000 Gift Voucher, it is treated as an instant prize to be redeemed at our office.

4. January 2027 Intake Lucky Draw: If you land on “You’ve Entered the Lucky Draw!”, your details are recorded separately in the official Lucky Draw pool for the January 2027 intake.

5. Final Premium Gift Draw: Students from the Lucky Draw pool who later receive their visa through Success Education & Visa Services will be eligible for the final draw featuring 3 Premium Gifts / Prizes.

6. Wheel Slots & Distribution: The wheel features 12 equal slots consisting of 3 Lucky Draw Entries, 2 Rs. 1,000 Gift Vouchers, 4 Rs. 500 Gift Vouchers, and 3 Better Luck Next Time slots.
